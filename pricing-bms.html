BMS FAQ

Common questions from building committees

Every flat or unit registered in your building counts, whether occupied or vacant. If you have 48 occupied and 4 vacant, you're billed for 52 units. This ensures all units are always tracked — vacant units can be listed for rent or sale directly from Pulse.

The admin handles everything from their end. Residents who prefer cash or in-person payment do so as usual — the admin logs it in Pulse and a receipt is generated. WhatsApp reminders are sent from the admin's account, so residents receive them without needing to install anything.

Most buildings go live in under 24 hours. Our onboarding team helps you import your resident list, configure billing amounts, and invite your first stakeholders in a single guided session. If you're migrating from Excel or WhatsApp, we help transfer the historical data too.
